Job Specs

Job Overview:

We’re looking for a passionate and skilled Web Developer to join our growing team at Hot Beans Web. In this role, you’ll be responsible for creating and maintaining innovative websites that deliver exceptional user experiences. From building responsive designs to optimising performance, you’ll work on a variety of exciting projects for diverse clients. If you have a strong foundation in web development, enjoy problem-solving, and thrive in a collaborative environment, this is the perfect opportunity for you.

  • Proficiency in front end programming (HTML, CSS, JavaScript)
  • Solid understanding in web development practices
  • Strong communication skills with the ability to work together and independently
  • A portfolio of previous projects you have taken part in
